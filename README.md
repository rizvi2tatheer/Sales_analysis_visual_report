# Automobile Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses automobile sales data to show where sales come from, which products sell best, and who the key buyers are. A short PowerPoint storyboard presents the findings.

## Objective
Turn raw sales data into a clear, easy-to-read dashboard that supports business decisions.

## Tools Used
- Power BI (data modelling, DAX measures, visuals, slicers)
- PowerPoint (storyboard of insights)

## What I Did
1. Loaded the sales dataset into Power BI and checked it for errors and missing values.
2. Created measures for total sales and predicted selling price by buyer.
3. Built charts matched to the data:
   - Bar chart: sales by country
   - Donut chart: sales by deal size
   - Column charts: predicted selling price and quantity ordered by product line
   - Clustered bar chart: sales vs predicted price by buyer
4. Added **Status** and **Product Line** slicers so every chart can be filtered.
5. Kept the design clean: one main colour, clear titles, data labels, and no clutter.
6. Wrote the key takeaways and business recommendations into a short presentation.

## Key Insights
- **Geography:** The USA brings in about 35% of total sales (3.4M), almost 3x more than Spain.
- **Deal size:** Medium deals drive about 60% of sales (5.9M).
- **Products:** Classic Cars lead in both value (about 41%) and volume (33K of 97K units). Trains are the weakest line.
- **Buyers:** Euro Shopping Channel and Mini Gifts Distributors make up about 16% of sales. Predicted price closely matches actual sales for every buyer.

## Recommendations
- Protect the US market and test growth in Spain and France.
- Upsell small deals into medium bundles.
- Keep Classic and Vintage Cars well stocked and review the Trains line.
- Retain key buyers and grow mid-tier accounts.
